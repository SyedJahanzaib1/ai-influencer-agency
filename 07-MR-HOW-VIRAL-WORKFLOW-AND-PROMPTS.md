## 🔄 3. Step-by-Step Production Workflow ($0 Cost)

### Step 1: Base Character Generation
- Tool: **Leonardo.ai** / **Tensor.art** / **SeaArt AI**.
- Pick the best face output and save it as `master_face_reference.png`.

### Step 2: Face Swapping & Consistency
- Tool: **Remaker AI Face Swap** (Remaker.ai/face-swap-free).
- Generate target lifestyle/outfit scenes, then swap `master_face_reference.png` onto them.

### Step 3: Motion & Reel Creation
- Tool: **Kling AI** / **Hailuo AI** / **Luma**.
- Prompt: `"Slow cinematic camera pan, natural facial expressions, subtle hair movement, ultra-realistic 4k motion."`

### Step 4: Urdu / English Voice & Lip Sync
- Tool: **ElevenLabs** (Urdu/Hindi/English voice) + **LivePortrait** / **Hedra**.
- Sync voice audio to the AI face clip.
