# 📩 Client Acquisition & Outreach Scripts

Use text-based outreach (Instagram DMs, LinkedIn Messages, Cold Emails) to secure brand clients without needing phone/video calls.

---

## Script 1: Instagram DM to E-Commerce / Fashion Brands

> **Subject / Opening:** Quick question about your upcoming catalog shoot!
>
> **Message:**
> "Hi [Brand Name] team! 👋
> 
> I really love your latest collection! I notice you regularly launch new apparel designs.
> 
> We run an AI-powered virtual model agency. We create photorealistic digital model shoots for brands, cutting physical photoshoot costs by over 75% while delivering catalog-ready HD photos and video reels in 24 hours.
> 
> I’d love to send you a **100% FREE sample shoot** using one of your actual products so you can see the quality firsthand. 
> 
> Can I send over a quick sample photo?"

---

## Script 2: Follow-up After "Yes"

> "Awesome! Please send over 1 or 2 clean product photos (on a flat lay or hanger).
> 
> I’ll have our virtual model wear your exact item and send the HD render back to you within 24 hours!"

---

## Script 3: Pitch Closing After Delivering Sample

> "Here is the sample shoot render! 📸
> 
> As you can see, the fabric texture and fit are preserved completely on our digital model. 
> 
> If you like this look, we can handle your entire next catalog (20 items + 5 Reels) for a flat rate of Rs 35,000 ($180). 
> 
> Would you be open to getting this set up for your next product launch?"
