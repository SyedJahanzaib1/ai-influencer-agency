# 💰 Monetization & Agency Pricing Strategy

## 1. Primary Business Models

### Model A: B2B E-Commerce & Brand Agency (Fastest Cashflow)
Provide virtual model shoots to local sportswear exporters, Shopify apparel stores, and jewelry brands who want high-end model photos without paying $2,000+ for physical photoshoots, models, and makeup artists.

### Model B: Owned AI Creator Page (Long-Term Asset)
Grow an Instagram/TikTok AI Influencer page. Monetize via:
- Brand sponsorships
- Affiliate product links in bio
- Fanvue / Patreon subscriptions
