# 🎬 Reels & Short-Video Production Workflow

Static photos build feed aesthetics; **Short-Form Videos (Reels / TikToks / Shorts)** bring the AI model to life and drive viral organic traffic.

---

## Step 1: Image-to-Video Generation
1. Go to **[Kling AI](https://klingai.com/)** or **[Hailuo AI](https://hailuoai.video/)**.
2. Select **Image to Video**.
3. Upload the face-swapped photo from the creation pipeline.
4. **Motion Prompt:** Keep motion minimal and natural for realism:
   > `Model smiling at the camera, gentle wind moving hair, natural eye blinking, camera slowly zooming in, 4k ultra-realistic motion.`
5. Export as a 5-second 1080p MP4 clip.

---

## Step 2: Adding Voiceover & Lip Sync (Optional)
If your AI influencer is a content creator giving tips/talking:
1. Generate voice audio using **[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/)**.
2. Upload audio and image to **[Hedra AI](https://hedra.com/)** or **[LivePortrait]**.
3. Export the synchronized video.

---

## Step 3: Final Editing & Sound Design
1. Import video into **CapCut**.
2. Add trending audio or Instagram background sound.
3. Add auto-captions if speaking.
4. Export at **1080x1920 (9:16 aspect ratio)**, 60fps.
